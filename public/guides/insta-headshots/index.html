How AI Headshot Generators Work, Generally

Nearly every tool in this category follows the same basic flow:

  1. Upload a handful of selfies (most ask for 10 or more, in varying lighting and angles).
  2. AI renders your portraits — the service trains or fine-tunes a model on your face, then generates images in various outfits and backdrops.
  3. Download your finished images, usually within minutes to a few hours depending on the plan.

The differences between services show up in the details: how many looks you get, how long generation takes, what happens to your photos afterward, and what the refund policy actually covers if something goes wrong.

What to Check Before You Pay

Before handing over selfies and a card number to any AI headshot service, look for three things stated plainly, not buried in a FAQ:

  • AI disclosure. The output is AI-generated. A responsible service says so and doesn't promise your photos will look exactly like you — resemblance varies, and no legitimate tool can guarantee an identical likeness.
  • Refund policy. Know whether refunds are automatic (tied to a delivery failure) or discretionary, and whether "I don't like how I look" counts as grounds for a refund. Usually it doesn't unless the batch produced almost nothing usable.
  • Privacy handling. Check whether each user gets their own isolated AI model, whether photos are ever shared or used to train models for other users, and how long data is retained before deletion. A privacy policy that doesn't answer this directly is a reason to hesitate.
